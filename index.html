Quick guide

  • Reveal squares to find safe ground.
  • A number tells you how many mines touch that square, including diagonals.
  • Right-click (or long-press isn't required; use a mouse) to flag a suspected mine.
  • Reveal every safe square to win. Your first reveal is always safe.
